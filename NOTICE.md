## Production-data boundary

Prior presentation work informed the engineering casebook: source-coordinate registration, coherent structural parts, restrained motion, readiness before navigation, latest-intent handling, accessibility, and separate production data. None of that project's event copy, schedules, portraits, logos, QR payloads, correspondence, or source/runtime artwork is included here.

The supplied example is an original composition of rectangles and ellipses, with no raster assets or named real-world subject. Image parser tests contain only a one-pixel synthetic fixture and constructed format headers.
